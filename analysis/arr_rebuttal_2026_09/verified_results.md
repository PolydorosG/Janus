## Inference conventions

20,000 paired scenario bootstrap draws; percentile 95% intervals. 200,000 two-sided sign-flip draws; BH adjustment separately over 12 models for each ordering variant. These are sensitivity checks, not a new confirmatory family. Pooled ordering intervals resample the 160 scenarios, retaining models together. Missing comparisons are excluded, never imputed as zero. Complete-both analyses condition on model outputs and are robustness subsets, not causal estimates for all scenarios. Numeric polarity summaries count source fact/value occurrences and require the value in a matched unit; they are diagnostic, not semantically verified numeric attribution. Original caches and manuscript are not modified.
